Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how BoutonFlareX uses them on our website, and how you can control them. Cookies are small text files placed on your device that help websites function properly and understand how they're being used. We use a limited set of cookies, each serving a specific, identifiable purpose described below.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for our website to function correctly, including keeping items in your shopping cart, remembering your session while you browse, and supporting basic security features. Because they're essential to the site working at all, they cannot be switched off through our own cookie settings, though you can still block them through your browser at the cost of some site functionality.

Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies, including those associated with Google's gtag.js tracking, to understand aggregate patterns in how visitors use our site — which pages are viewed most, how people navigate between them, and which content is genuinely useful. This information is used only in aggregate and helps us make practical improvements to the site over time.

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember choices you've made on our site, such as a previously selected size guide preference or a dismissed notification, so you don't have to make the same choice again on your next visit within a reasonable window of time.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most browsers allow you to view, manage, and delete cookies through their settings menu, and you can choose to block some or all cookies if you prefer, understanding that this may affect certain site features.
