Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BoutonFlareX collects, uses and protects the personal information you share with us when you browse our site, place an order, sign up for our newsletter, or contact our team. We've written it in plain language because we think a privacy policy should actually be readable, not just legally present on the page. If anything here is unclear after reading it, our team is genuinely happy to explain it further by phone or email using the details at the bottom of this page.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, create an account, or sign up for our newsletter, we collect information such as your name, shipping and billing address, email address, and phone number, along with basic order details like items purchased and order value. We also collect limited technical information automatically when you browse our site, including your approximate location, browser type, and the pages you visit, which helps us understand how the site is used and where it can be improved.

We do not collect more information than we reasonably need to fulfill an order, respond to an inquiry, or operate the site securely, and we periodically review what we collect to make sure that remains true as our systems change.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to process and ship your orders, respond to questions you send through our contact form, send you the newsletter if you've opted in, and improve the site based on how it's actually being used. We may also use your contact details to let you know about a genuine issue with an order, such as a shipping delay or a sizing question our team has about your request.

We do not use your personal information to make automated decisions that would meaningfully affect you without a person being involved, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Sharing Your Information

We share information with service providers who help us operate the business, such as payment processors and shipping carriers, only to the extent needed for them to perform that specific service. These providers are contractually required to protect your information and use it only for the purpose we've engaged them for, not for their own independent marketing.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of BoutonFlareX, our customers, or the public.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep items in your cart between visits, and understand aggregate site usage through analytics tools such as Google Analytics. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time, though some parts of the site may not function as smoothly if you do.

Please see our separate Cookie Policy for a more detailed explanation of the specific categories of cookies we use and why each one is set.

Your Choices and Rights

You can update or correct your account information at any time by contacting our team directly, and you can unsubscribe from our newsletter using the link included in every email we send. Depending on where you live, you may also have additional rights regarding your personal information, such as requesting a copy of the data we hold about you or asking us to delete it.

To exercise any of these rights, simply reach out using the contact details below, and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe, typically within a few business days.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us at hello@boutonflarex.com, by phone at +1-888-777-5845, or by mail at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. We update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law, and the date of the most recent update is always available upon request.
